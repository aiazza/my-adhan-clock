Masjid sync (optional)

When this is set, the masjid's iqamah times replace the ones in the table below. A browser only loads mawaqit.net directly if the kiosk allows cross-site requests; otherwise point this at a JSON feed you host.

Prayers

PrayerAdhan soundIqamahAdjust (min)

Iqamah takes minutes after the adhan (15) or a fixed time (19:30). Leave it empty to hide it. Adjust shifts the adhan time to match your masjid's timetable.

Adhan audio

With no recording chosen, the clock looks for adhan.mp3 and adhan-fajr.mp3 next to this file, then falls back to a soft chime.
